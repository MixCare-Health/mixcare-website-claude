Additional charges & notes

  • Claim fee: 7% of approved claim amount, charged if MixCare provides claim adjudication and bank remittance services.
  • Member definition: Each "member" corresponds to one individual employee or dependent with platform access.
  • Pro-ration: Annual subscription fees are pro-rated from the member's enrollment date to the end of the 12-month contract. Upon renewal, fees are charged in full for each subsequent 12-month term. In the event of early termination, fees are pro-rated to the termination date with no refund on amounts already paid.
  • Wellness budget: MixCare charges the Employer for the Wellness Budget strictly based on the actual usage of eligible employees.
  • HealthCoin Pre-fund Pool: Client settles 50% of the total employee wellness budget as a "HealthCoin Pre-fund Pool" within 15 business days of the launch Effective Date.
