The Challenge

四大賽項，讓日常變得有趣

Every activity tracked on the MixCare Health App contributes to your team's cumulative score across four measurable dimensions of health.

Move

追蹤每日步數。目標達到 10,000 步。每日最高單項積分為20分。

Rest

每晚透過應用程式記錄睡眠。休息恢復同樣計入積分。

見字飲水

記錄每日飲水量。簡單的習慣，每天為您累積積分。

Nourish

Log your meals through the MixCare Health App. Nutrition tracking counts toward your daily score alongside activity.

Daily Target & Catch-up

The daily target is 50 points, but up to 70–80 points are earnable each day — so missing your step goal never ends your day. Catch up through food, water, meditation and other healthy habits.

HRA Bonus

Complete the Health Risk Assessment once at the start for a one-off 50-point bonus — outside the daily cap.

Over 21 days at full effort — earning up to 70–80 points a day plus the one-off HRA bonus — a participant can accumulate roughly 1,700 points, with every tracked activity from day one shaping their final leaderboard position.

Competition Formats

Choose your category.

Three tiers let everyone compete at the level that fits — from individuals and squads to startups, SMEs and full corporates.

Individual + Squad

Compete solo or pair up. Rank as an individual within your company, or team up in a squad of two for a shared score — the most personal, internally competitive tier.

Startup / SME

Smaller companies compete on a level playing field. Startups and SMEs are ranked against organisations of similar size — so a lean team never has to race a corporate giant.

Corporate

Large organisations go head-to-head. Full company scores compete against other corporates — the tier that drives the highest company-wide participation.
